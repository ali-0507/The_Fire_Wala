WHY AMC MATTERS

Risks If AMC Is Not Taken

Without regular maintenance, fire safety systems may not perform when they are needed most.

System Failure

The fire safety system may fail during an emergency.

NOC Delays

Fire NOC renewal or re-inspection may get delayed.

Insurance Issues

Insurance claims may face issues due to missing maintenance records.

Legal Penalties

Legal fines or non-maintenance observations may occur.

Safety & Property Risk

Risk to life, property, and reputation may increase.

Silent Equipment Failure

Pumps, panels, valves, detectors, and extinguishers may fail silently.

BEFORE WE START

Our Pre-AMC Inspection Process

We inspect your site first, understand your requirements, and then prepare the right AMC plan.

Engineer Site Visit

Our engineer visits your site and checks the existing fire safety system.

  • System and equipment condition
  • Working and non-working equipment
  • Brands and installation details
  • Previous AMC details, if available

Inspection Report

A detailed report is prepared based on the actual site inspection.

  • Repair and replacement points
  • Photos as evidence
  • Upgrade suggestions
  • Safety risk points

AMC Quotation

The quotation is prepared according to your actual site condition and requirements.

AMC Cost Maintenance + Repairs + Quantity + Visits + Distance + Documentation + GST

Visit Charges

Visit charges depend on the site requirements and AMC decision.

  • AMC taken → Visit may be free or adjustable
  • AMC declined → Visit will be chargeable
  • Depends on system, building size and distance
Inspect first. Plan better. We understand your site before preparing your AMC plan.
