Fire Extinguisher Supply & Refilling Services

Fire extinguishers are the first line of defence during a fire emergency. Simply having an extinguisher installed is not enough—it must remain in proper working condition. Over time, extinguishers may face issues such as:

  • Expired service validity
  • Low pressure
  • Leakage,Corrosion
  • Damaged hose, nozzle or handle
  • Poor extinguishing-agent condition

Regular inspection, maintenance and refilling help keep extinguishers operational, maintained and ready for emergency use.

Types of risky materials

Different fire risks need different protection

A

Class A extinguisher

Ordinary combustibles such as Wood, paper, rubber and plastics are typical examples.

B

Class B extinguisher

Flammable liquids such as Fuels, paints, solvents and other flammable-liquid hazards.

C

Class C extinguisher

Flammable gases under pressure require appropriate protection.

D

Class D extinguisher

Specialised equipment may be needed for metal fire hazards.

F

Class F extinguisher

Relevant to cooking-oil and fat fire hazards.

ABC Dry Powder

Our visual service process

What actually happens

Kindly, click on each step to change the visual.

01

Requirement Collection

We verify the extinguisher quantity, type, capacity and site requirements.

02

Physician Inspection

Every extinguisher is checked for physical condition, corrosion, hose, nozzle, handle and safety components.

03

Old agent removal

Existing extinguishing agent is removed where applicable and the cylinder is cleaned before refilling.

04

Refilling

The extinguisher is refilled with the appropriate extinguishing agent according to its type.

05

Pressure charging and quality check

Pressure, charging and leakage conditions are checked before the extinguisher is returned to service.

06

Sticker and tagging

Each serviced extinguisher is identified with service and next-due information.

07

Reinstallation

After servicing and verification, extinguishers are returned to their designated locations.

08

Documentation

Service records, quantity summaries and applicable deficiency observations are submitted to the client.
